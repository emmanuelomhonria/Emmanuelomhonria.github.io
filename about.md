I hold the words "pastor" and "team lead" in the same hand, and I've
stopped apologizing for that. <!-- TODO: rewrite this opening in your own voice -->

For most of a decade I've led a team spread across Nigeria, Canada, and
the Philippines at an international tech company — running sprints,
resolving disputes, standing as the bridge between leadership and the
people doing the work. On Sundays, and most days in between, I shepherd
people through a different kind of dispute: the one between who they
are and who they're becoming.

I earned an MBA from Nexford University with first class honors, and a
BSc from the University of Benin before that. But the credential that
actually shows up in a Tuesday afternoon customer escalation, or a
Sunday sermon, is the same one: integrity, held under pressure, in
public.

## What I believe

- **Character is a business asset, not a soft skill.** Teams and
  congregations both run on trust, and trust is built in the same way
  in either room.
- **Discipline is love with structure.** Whether it's a sprint
  deadline or a spiritual habit, the discipline is what makes the care
  durable.
- **Leadership is a bridge, not a pedestal.** My job — with my team,
  with my church — is to stand between two groups and make sure both
  are actually heard.
