I lead a team on weekdays and a congregation on Sundays, and for a long
time I treated those as two different jobs requiring two different
people. They aren't, and it isn't.

> The test of character isn't what you do when everyone's watching.
> It's what you do when a refund request lands on your desk and no one
> would ever know if you cut a corner.

This blog is where I write both halves down — reflections on faith and
character, and notes on leading a real team with real deadlines. If
you're here for one and stay for the other, that's the point.

## What to expect

- Short, honest posts — not sermons dressed up as blog posts
- Real situations from leading a distributed team
- Occasional updates on the hiring agency I'm building

Glad you're here.
